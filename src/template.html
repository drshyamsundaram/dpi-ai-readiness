How to answer

  • 1, 3 and 5 have written anchors. Choose 2 or 4 when the truth lies between two anchors.
  • Score what operates at national scale today, not what has been announced or piloted.
  • Choose DK if you don't know. It isn't scored, but two or more DKs in a pillar mark it as having insufficient evidence.
  • Add one fact or source for each answer. Scores without evidence are counted, but flagged.
  • The {{COUNTRY}} baseline is hidden while you answer, so it doesn't anchor your scores. You can switch it on in the sidebar.
Full guidance is in Instructions; the method is in ReadMe.

The eight pillars

P1–P3 are the rails India built for DPI. P4–P6 are what AI adds on top. P7–P8 decide whether the system is trusted and used.
