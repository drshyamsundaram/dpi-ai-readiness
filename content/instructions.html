Instructions

For a single respondent it takes about 25 minutes. For a facilitated group, plan about a week before the session to collect responses.

For respondents

  1. Read the core pre-reads. The six starred items in References take about six hours.
  2. Fill in your profile on the Start tab: your name, organisation, the country you're assessing, your role and your familiarity with India's DPI.
  3. Answer each question on the Assess tab.
    • Read the three descriptions and pick the score that best matches the evidence you know.
    • Choose 2 or 4 when the truth lies between two descriptions.
    • Score what operates at national scale today, not what has been announced or piloted.
    • Choose DK if you don't know, rather than guessing.
    • Add one fact, source or example in the evidence box.
  4. Leave the {{COUNTRY}} baseline off while you answer, so it doesn't sway your scores. Switch it on afterwards to compare.
  5. Review your results on the Results tab: overall score, band, binding constraint, gaps and the questions to bring to the session.
  6. Export your response as JSON and send the file to the facilitator. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

For facilitators

  1. Share this file (or its hosted link) with participants one to two weeks before the session, with the core pre-reads.
  2. Collect the JSON exports. Ask everyone to name their file after themselves.
  3. Load all files on the Group tab by dropping them in together.
  4. Start with contested pillars. Where the spread is above 1.0, compare the evidence people gave before debating priorities.
  5. Agree the binding constraint. Use the lowest-scoring pillar and the largest gap to the {{COUNTRY}} baseline as starting points.
  6. Close with a first move. Agree one action, an owner and a 12-month marker for the weakest pillar.
  7. Export the group CSV for the session record.

Reading the results

Tips

  • Use Print / PDF on the Results tab to save a one-page record.
  • The theme button (◐) switches between light and dark mode.
  • To start again, use Reset my answers on the Start tab.
